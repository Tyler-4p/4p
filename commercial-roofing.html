Commercial Roof Systems

The right system depends on the building, its roof slope, and your budget. Many commercial buildings have low-slope or flat roofs, and the systems covered on our flat roofing page, such as TPO, EPDM, and Flintlastic peel-and-stick, are commonly used on them. Others are better suited to metal roofing.

We'll go over the options for your property and explain the tradeoffs, so you can decide with confidence.

Signs Your Commercial Roof Needs Attention

  • Leaks or water stains on ceilings and walls inside the building
  • Standing water that doesn't drain after a rain
  • Bubbling, blistering, or cracked and separated seams
  • Damaged or loose flashing around vents, walls, and rooftop equipment
  • Visible damage after a hailstorm or high winds
  • A roof that's getting older and hasn't been inspected in a while

Catching a problem early is usually far less costly than waiting, since water that gets in can damage the deck, insulation, and everything below it.
