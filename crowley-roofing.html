What Crowley Roofs Are Up Against

Crowley sits right in the path of the spring storms that roll across southern Tarrant County. These are the roof problems we see most often.

Hail Damage

Hail cracks and bruises shingles and strips away the protective granules. The roof may not leak right away, but its lifespan drops — and most of the damage can't be seen from the ground.

Wind Damage and Missing Shingles

Strong gusts can lift, crease, and tear off shingles. Once the seal breaks, rain can work its way under the roof and into the decking.

Builder-Grade Roofs in Newer Subdivisions

Many of Crowley's newer homes came with basic shingles and limited attic ventilation. Replacement is the perfect time to upgrade to impact-resistant shingles and better ventilation.

Heat and Ventilation Problems

Texas summers push attic temperatures sky-high. Poor ventilation shortens shingle life and raises energy bills, so we check ventilation on every roof replacement.

How to Choose a Roofing Contractor in Crowley

After a hailstorm, Crowley neighborhoods often fill up with out-of-town roofing crews. Whether you hire us or someone else, these checks will protect you.

  • Choose a local roofing company. A roofer based nearby will be around for warranty questions and follow-up long after the storm crews leave.
  • Verify insurance. Ask for a current insurance certificate before any work begins.
  • Get an itemized written estimate. A good roofing estimate lists materials, labor, decking, and cleanup — not just one total price.
  • Ask about the workmanship warranty. Know how long it lasts and how it works with the manufacturer's shingle warranty.
  • Avoid deductible schemes. In Texas it's illegal for a roofer to pay, waive, or rebate your insurance deductible.
  • Read Google reviews from Crowley homeowners. Local reviews tell you more than any sales pitch at the door.

Crowley Roofing FAQ

How much does a roof replacement cost in Crowley, TX?

Roof replacement cost in Crowley depends on the size and pitch of your roof, the shingle or metal you choose, and whether any decking needs replacing. We give you a written, itemized estimate after a free roof inspection so you know the exact cost before work begins.

Should I repair or replace my roof?

It depends on the age of the roof and how widespread the damage is. A few missing shingles or a single leak can often be repaired. Widespread hail damage or an aging roof usually makes replacement the better value. We show you photos and explain both options.

How do I know if my Crowley roof has hail damage?

Look for dented gutters, vents, or downspouts, and granules collecting at the bottom of downspouts. Most hail damage to shingles can't be seen from the ground, so a free roof inspection is the best way to know for sure.

What should I do if my roof is leaking?

Move belongings away from the drip, catch the water, and call us for an inspection. We find the actual source of the leak, which is often flashing, a vent boot, or storm damage, rather than just patching the stain you can see.

Are impact-resistant shingles worth it in Crowley?

For many North Texas homeowners, impact-resistant shingles are worth considering because of hail risk. Some insurance companies offer discounts for them, so it's worth asking your agent. We'll walk you through the options during your inspection.

Can a roofer pay my insurance deductible?

No. In Texas it is illegal for a roofing contractor to pay, waive, or rebate a homeowner's insurance deductible. Any roofer who offers is a red flag.
