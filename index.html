Confidence in Your Contractor. Pride in Your Roof.

Locally Owned. Personally Invested.

4P Roofing is based in Burleson and serves homeowners throughout the surrounding DFW communities. This is where we live, work, and build our reputation.

Honest Recommendations You Can Understand

You shouldn't have to understand roofing terminology to make a good decision. We explain what we find, share inspection reports along with our 21-point inspection, and walk you through your options. If a repair is appropriate, we recommend it. If replacement makes more sense, we explain why so you can decide with confidence.

Attention to the Details Beneath the Shingles

A dependable roof takes more than attractive shingles. Decking, underlayment, flashing, ventilation, and other components all play a role in protecting your home. We evaluate those details and explain what your project requires, with a scope that addresses the roofing system as a whole.

Experienced Crews We Know and Trust

The people installing your roof matter as much as the material being installed. We work with experienced crews we know and trust, and we set clear expectations for workmanship, care of your property, and cleanup. We take pride in delivering a finished project you can feel good about.

Clear Pricing and Communication

You deserve to know what you're paying for before work begins. We provide a written scope, explain material options and payment expectations, and keep you informed as your project moves forward. If hidden damage is discovered during the removal, we document it and discuss the necessary work and costs with you.

Protection Backed by a Written Warranty

Our roof replacements include a 5-year workmanship warranty under its written terms. We explain coverage alongside the applicable manufacturer warranty, so you understand how your investment is protected and who to contact if a concern comes up.
