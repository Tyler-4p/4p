What Cleburne Roofs Are Up Against

Weather in Johnson County doesn't go easy on a roof. Here's what we see most often around Cleburne.

Hail Across Open Country

Storms that roll across open land around Cleburne can bring hail and strong straight-line winds. Damage often can't be seen from the yard, so a check after a big storm is smart.

Wind on Exposed Properties

Homes and buildings on acreage don't have much to block the wind. Gusts can lift shingles, loosen metal panel fasteners, and open the door for leaks.

Aging Roofs on Older Homes

Cleburne has plenty of homes with years of history behind them. Older roofs often hide worn flashing, past patch jobs, and soft decking that only shows up at tear-off.

Heat and Poor Ventilation

Texas summers push attic temperatures way up. Without good ventilation, shingles wear out faster and cooling bills climb. We check ventilation on every job.

How to Pick a Roofer in Cleburne (Even If It's Not Us)

When a storm hits Johnson County, it's common to see unfamiliar trucks going door to door within days. A few simple checks can save you a lot of trouble.

  • Make sure they're local to the area. Ask where the company is based. A nearby roofer can come back if something needs attention later.
  • See their insurance certificate. A legitimate roofer won't hesitate to show it.
  • Read the scope before you sign. It should list materials, labor, decking replacement, and cleanup in writing.
  • Ask how the warranty works. Find out what workmanship warranty they offer and who you call if a problem comes up.
  • Watch for deductible offers. Texas law makes it illegal for a roofer to pay, waive, or rebate your insurance deductible.
  • Look for reviews from your neighbors. Google reviews from Cleburne and Johnson County homeowners tell you more than any sales pitch.
