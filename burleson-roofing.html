What Burleson Roofs Are Up Against

North Texas is tough on roofs. Knowing what to watch for helps you catch small problems before they turn into big ones.

Hail and Spring Storms

Hail bruises shingles and knocks off the granules that protect them — often without damage you can see from the ground. After a storm, it's worth having your roof checked.

Summer Heat and Sun

Long, hot summers bake shingles and dry out sealants around vents and flashing. Good attic ventilation makes a big difference in how long a roof lasts.

Builder-Grade Roofs on Newer Homes

Many newer homes were built with entry-level materials and minimal ventilation. Replacement is a chance to upgrade shingles and fix ventilation the right way.

Hidden Decking Damage

Slow leaks can rot the decking long before you see a ceiling stain. That's why we tear off to bare wood and replace bad decking instead of covering it up.

How to Pick a Roofer in Burleson (Even If It's Not Us)

After a big storm, out-of-town crews often knock on doors across Burleson. Here's how to protect yourself.

  • Look for a local address. A company based nearby will still be around if something comes up later.
  • Ask for proof of insurance. Any reputable roofer will gladly show you their insurance certificate.
  • Get everything in writing. The estimate should spell out materials, scope of work, and whether decking replacement is included.
  • Never let anyone "cover" your deductible. In Texas it's illegal for a roofing contractor to pay, waive, or rebate your insurance deductible.
  • Don't sign on the spot. A good roofer will give you time to read the contract and compare. High-pressure "today only" deals are a red flag.
  • Check real reviews. Look at Google reviews from homeowners in your area, not just testimonials on a website.

Burleson Roofing Questions

How long does a roof replacement take in Burleson?

Most residential roof replacements take one to two days, depending on the size of the roof, the material, and the weather. We give you a timeline before work starts.

How do I know if my roof has hail damage?

Hail damage is often hard to see from the ground. Common signs include dented gutters, vents, or downspouts, granules collecting at downspouts, and dark spots on shingles. A free inspection with photos is the easiest way to know for sure.

Do I need to be home during the inspection or the install?

For the inspection, we prefer to walk through the findings with you, but we can send photos if you can't be there. For the install, you don't need to be home, though we'll do a final walkthrough with you when it's done.

Can a roofer pay or waive my insurance deductible?

No. In Texas it is illegal for a roofing contractor to pay, waive, or rebate a homeowner's insurance deductible. Any contractor offering to do that is a red flag.
