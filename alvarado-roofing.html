What Alvarado Roofs Are Up Against

Southern Johnson County gets its share of rough weather. These are the issues we watch for most on Alvarado roofs.

Hail From Spring Storms

Hailstones can crack and bruise shingles without leaving damage you'd notice from the driveway. Over time, that lost protection leads to leaks and a shorter roof life.

Wind With Nothing to Stop It

Open land around Alvarado lets storm winds build speed. Strong gusts can break shingle seals, peel back edges, and work metal panel fasteners loose.

Builder-Grade Roofs on New Homes

Many of Alvarado's newer homes came with basic shingles and minimal ventilation. When replacement time comes, it's a chance to upgrade to something tougher.

Attic Heat in Texas Summers

A poorly vented attic traps heat that cooks shingles from underneath and drives up energy bills. We check and improve ventilation as part of every replacement.

How to Pick a Roofer in Alvarado (Even If It's Not Us)

After hail moves through, Alvarado neighborhoods often fill up with door knockers and yard signs from companies no one's heard of. Here's how to tell who's worth your time.

  • Find out where they're based. A roofer with roots nearby can come back if you ever need them. A crew from out of state may be long gone.
  • Ask to see insurance. Any trustworthy contractor will hand over proof without hesitation.
  • Get the full scope in writing. Materials, labor, decking, and cleanup should all be listed before you agree to anything.
  • Understand the warranty. Ask what workmanship coverage you get and who handles it if something goes wrong.
  • Turn down deductible deals. In Texas, it's against the law for a roofer to pay, waive, or rebate your insurance deductible.
  • Read reviews from people nearby. Google reviews from Alvarado and Johnson County homeowners are your best gauge of real-world work.
