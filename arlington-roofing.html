What Arlington Roofs Are Up Against

Arlington sits in the middle of the Metroplex, and storms that sweep across DFW often pass right over it. Here's what we see most on Arlington roofs.

Hailstorms Across the Metroplex

A single DFW hailstorm can hit thousands of Arlington homes in one afternoon. The damage is easy to miss from the ground but shortens the life of your shingles.

Aging Roofs in Established Neighborhoods

Many Arlington homes have been standing for decades. Older roofs can hide worn flashing, layered repairs, and soft decking that only shows at tear-off.

Wind-Driven Rain

Strong spring storms can push rain sideways under lifted shingles and around flashing, causing leaks that show up long after the storm passes.

Summer Heat Buildup

Arlington's hot summers can bake an attic. Without proper ventilation, shingles age faster and your AC works harder. We check ventilation on every replacement.

How to Pick a Roofer in Arlington (Even If It's Not Us)

After a big hailstorm, Arlington can see dozens of roofing companies show up overnight. Before you sign with anyone, run through this list.

  • Check that they're based in the area. A local roofer is easy to reach for warranty questions. A storm crew that leaves town is not.
  • Request proof of insurance. Reputable contractors share their certificate without any pushback.
  • Get a detailed written scope. It should list materials, labor, decking, and cleanup — not just a single price.
  • Know the warranty terms. Ask what workmanship warranty is included and who handles it.
  • Say no to deductible deals. Texas law prohibits roofers from paying, waiving, or rebating your insurance deductible.
  • Read local reviews. Look for Google reviews from Arlington homeowners, not just ratings on the company's own website.
