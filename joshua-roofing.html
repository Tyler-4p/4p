What Joshua Roofs Are Up Against

Northern Johnson County sees its share of severe weather. These are the roof problems we run into most often in Joshua.

Hail Damage

Spring hailstorms crack and bruise shingles and knock off their protective granules. Most hail damage can't be seen from the ground but shortens your roof's life.

Wind Damage and Lifted Shingles

Strong gusts across open land can lift and crease shingles and break their seal, letting wind-driven rain work its way underneath.

Builder-Grade Roofs on New Homes

Many of Joshua's newer homes came with entry-level shingles and minimal ventilation. When it's time to replace, it's a chance to upgrade to impact-resistant shingles.

Heat and Attic Ventilation

Texas summers push attic temperatures way up. Poor ventilation shortens shingle life and raises energy bills, so we check it on every roof replacement.

How to Choose a Roofing Contractor in Joshua

After a hailstorm, Joshua's newer neighborhoods are often the first places out-of-town roofing crews go door to door. Whether you hire us or someone else, use this checklist.

  • Hire a local roofing contractor. A roofer based in Johnson County will be around for warranty questions long after storm crews leave.
  • Verify insurance. Ask for a current certificate of insurance before any work begins.
  • Get an itemized written estimate. It should list materials, labor, decking, ventilation, and cleanup — not just one number.
  • Understand the warranty. Ask how long the workmanship warranty lasts and how it works with the shingle manufacturer's warranty.
  • Avoid deductible schemes. In Texas it's illegal for a roofer to pay, waive, or rebate your insurance deductible.
  • Check Joshua reviews. Read Google reviews from Joshua and Johnson County homeowners before you sign.

Do you install metal roofs on shops and barns around Joshua?

Yes. Along with homes, we install metal roofing on shops, barns, and outbuildings on properties around Joshua.

Should I repair or replace my roof?

A few missing shingles or a single leak can often be repaired. Widespread hail damage or an aging roof usually makes replacement the better value. We show you photos and explain both options honestly.

Can a roofer pay my insurance deductible?

No. In Texas it is illegal for a roofing contractor to pay, waive, or rebate a homeowner's insurance deductible. Any roofer who offers is a red flag.
