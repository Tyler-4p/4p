What Godley Roofs Are Up Against

Out in western Johnson County, there's not much between your roof and an incoming storm. These are the problems we see most often on Godley properties.

Hail on Open Land

Hailstorms sweep across open pasture with nothing to slow them down. Hail cracks shingles, strips granules, and dents metal panels — often without obvious signs from the ground.

High Winds

Strong gusts can lift shingle edges, break seals, and loosen fasteners on metal roofs, creating openings for leaks on homes and outbuildings alike.

Aging Barn and Shop Roofs

Older metal roofs on barns and shops develop rust, loose screws, and worn sealant over time. Catching it early can prevent leaks that damage equipment and feed.

Summer Heat

Long Texas summers bake shingles and push attic temperatures up. Good ventilation helps your roof last longer and keeps cooling costs down.

How to Choose a Roofing Contractor in Godley

In a small town, word gets around — good and bad. After a storm, here's how to make sure you're hiring a roofer you can trust.

  • Hire a local roofer. A company based in Johnson County will be there for warranty questions long after storm crews leave.
  • Verify insurance. Ask for a current insurance certificate before work begins.
  • Get an itemized written estimate. It should spell out materials, labor, decking, and cleanup.
  • Know the warranty. Ask how long the workmanship warranty lasts and how it works with the manufacturer warranty.
  • Avoid deductible deals. In Texas it's illegal for a roofer to pay, waive, or rebate your insurance deductible.
  • Ask your neighbors. Local referrals and Google reviews from Godley and Johnson County homeowners say a lot.

How much does a roof replacement cost in Godley, TX?

Roof replacement cost depends on the size and pitch of the roof, the shingles or metal you choose, and how much decking needs replacing. After a free roof inspection, we give you a written, itemized estimate so you know the full cost up front.

How do I know if hail damaged my roof?

Look for dents in gutters, vents, or metal panels, and shingle granules collecting at downspouts. Most hail damage to shingles can't be seen from the ground, so a free roof inspection is the best way to know.

Can a roofer pay my insurance deductible?

No. In Texas it is illegal for a roofing contractor to pay, waive, or rebate a homeowner's insurance deductible. Any roofer who offers is a red flag.
