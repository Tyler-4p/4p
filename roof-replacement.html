What a Roof Replacement Includes

A full replacement isn't a patch on top of an old roof. We build a brand new roofing system from the ground up: damaged decking replaced, full synthetic underlayment installed, and dimensional shingles laid to manufacturer spec.

  • A full tear-off down to bare decking
  • Rotted or soft decking replaced before anything new goes down
  • Full synthetic underlayment across the roof
  • Dimensional shingles installed to manufacturer spec
  • Proper ridge ventilation and clean flashing
  • Clear expectations for workmanship, care of your property, and cleanup

01Everything Comes Off

Old shingles, old felt, all of it — stripped down to bare decking. Any rotted or soft boards get replaced before anything new goes down. We don't build on top of a problem.

02The Roof Gets Sealed

A full synthetic underlayment goes down next — every seam, every fastener point sealed tight. This is the layer that keeps water out long before a single shingle is laid.

03Then We Finish It Right

Dimensional shingles installed to manufacturer spec — correct nailing pattern and clean flashing. Every roof we replace gets an upgrade to Class 3 impact-rated shingles. We also make sure the ventilation is the right amount for the size of your home, so your roof and attic can breathe the way they should.

This is the step most crews rush. We don't.

When a Roof Replacement Makes Sense

A replacement makes sense when the problems are widespread, or when the roof has simply reached the end of its useful life. Common signs include:

  • The roof is old. Many asphalt shingle roofs reach the end of their life somewhere around 20 to 30 years, depending on the shingle and conditions.
  • Storm damage covers large areas of the roof, such as hail or wind damage across several slopes.
  • Shingles are curling, cracking, or losing granules across much of the roof.
  • You're dealing with repeated leaks, or repairs keep adding up without solving the problem.
  • The decking underneath is soft, sagging, or damaged.

Not sure whether you need a replacement? If a repair is appropriate, we recommend it. If replacement makes more sense, we explain why so you can decide with confidence. You can also read about roof repair.
